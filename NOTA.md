# Nota ProWalkie / Notes

## Butang Volume Naik / Volume Up key
- BM: Tahan butang Volume Naik = sama seperti tekan butang cakap (lepas = berhenti).
  Hanya jalan bila skrin suara dibuka dan app di depan. Tidak jalan bila skrin mati atau app di latar.
  Kalau telefon hanya menukar volume tanpa memancarkan suara, beritahu pembangun (perlu kod asli Android).
- EN: Hold Volume Up = same as pressing the talk button (release = stop).
  Works only while the talk screen is open and the app is in front. Not with the screen off or app in background.

## Status online / offline
- BM: Berdasarkan degupan setiap 60 saat. Selepas app ditutup, status jadi offline dalam kira-kira 2 minit.
- EN: Based on a heartbeat every 60 s. After the app closes, status turns offline within about 2 minutes.

## Kuota Agora / Agora quota
- BM: 10,000 minit percuma sebulan untuk SEMUA pengguna. Dikira ikut masa setiap orang berada dalam channel,
  bukan masa bercakap. Kekal online juga dikira. App keluar sendiri selepas 15 minit senyap.
- EN: 10,000 free minutes per month for ALL users, counted by time each person is in a channel, not talk time.
  Staying online counts too. The app leaves by itself after 15 minutes of silence.
